How it works

From walkthrough to sold, in four steps.

01

Walkthrough & consult

We tour the property with you or your agent, then learn the buyer or guest you're after, the timeline and the budget.

02

Design plan

A room-by-room plan for furniture, palette, art and accessories, chosen to highlight your home's best features.

03

Install day

We deliver, place and style everything, down to the art, linens and greenery.

04

Photograph & launch

We photograph the finished space for your listing or rental, so it stands out the moment it goes live.
